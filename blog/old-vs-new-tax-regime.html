Old vs New Tax Regime: Which Should You Choose?

A simple way to decide between the two income-tax regimes.

The new regime offers lower slab rates and a higher standard deduction but removes most deductions like 80C, 80D and HRA.

If your total deductions are modest, the new regime usually wins. If you have home-loan interest, HRA and full 80C investments, the old regime may still save more.

Salaried employees can switch every year; business owners have restrictions on switching back and forth.
