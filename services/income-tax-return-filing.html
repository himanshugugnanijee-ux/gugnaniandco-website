Income Tax Return Filing Services by Gugnani & Co

Choosing the wrong ITR form or missing an income shown in your AIS is the most common reason for a defective-return notice. We match your Form 16, Form 26AS and Annual Information Statement line-by-line, compare the old and new tax regime for you, and claim every deduction you are legally entitled to—80C, 80D, HRA, home-loan interest and capital-gain exemptions.

Documents Required

  • PAN & Aadhaar
  • Form 16 / salary slips
  • Bank interest certificates and AIS
  • Capital gains statements from broker
  • Investment & insurance proofs

Our Process

  1. Free consultation to understand your requirement
  2. Document checklist shared on WhatsApp/email
  3. Preparation and review by a qualified CA
  4. Filing and acknowledgement shared with you
  5. Follow-up and reminders for future compliance

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to file ITR?

For non-audit individuals it is usually 31 July; belated returns can be filed until 31 December with late fee.

Which regime is better, old or new?

It depends on your deductions. We calculate both and file under the one that saves more tax.

Can you file ITR for NRIs?

Yes, including DTAA relief and Indian rental or capital-gain income.
