Tax Audit (Section 44AB) Services by Gugnani & Co

If your business turnover exceeds ₹1 crore (₹10 crore with mostly digital transactions) or your professional receipts exceed ₹50 lakh, a tax audit is mandatory. We verify books, prepare the 44 clauses of Form 3CD, and upload the audit report before the due date to save the 0.5% penalty under section 271B.

Documents Required

  • Complete books of accounts
  • Fixed asset register
  • GST returns
  • Loan and TDS details

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the due date of tax audit?

Generally 30 September of the assessment year.

What is the penalty for not getting audited?

0.5% of turnover up to ₹1.5 lakh.

Is tax audit needed under presumptive scheme?

Only in specific cases where lower profits are declared and income exceeds the basic exemption.
