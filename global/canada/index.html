FAQs – Canada

Federal or provincial?

Federal incorporation gives name protection across Canada; provincial can be simpler if you operate in one province.

What is the small supplier threshold?

Generally $30,000 of taxable supplies over four consecutive calendar quarters.

Do you work with my accountant?

Yes, we deliver year-end packages your Canadian accountant can rely on.

When is the T2 due?

Generally within six months of the corporation's year-end; tax balances are usually due earlier.
