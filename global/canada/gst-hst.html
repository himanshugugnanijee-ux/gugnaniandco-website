GST/HST Registration & Filing for Canada businesses

Businesses generally must register for GST/HST once taxable supplies exceed $30,000 in four consecutive quarters. We register your account, track input tax credits and prepare your periodic returns.

What's included

  • GST/HST registration
  • Input tax credit tracking
  • Periodic return preparation
  • Instalment reminders

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the small supplier threshold?

Generally $30,000 of taxable supplies over four consecutive calendar quarters.
