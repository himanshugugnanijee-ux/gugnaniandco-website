US LLC & Corporation Formation for United States businesses

Non-US residents can own a US LLC or corporation without visiting the country. We guide you on the right state and entity type, file the formation documents through a registered agent, draft your Operating Agreement or Bylaws and obtain the EIN. For Indian resident founders we also take care of the FEMA / ODI reporting that applies when you invest in an overseas entity.

  • FEMA / ODI compliance for Indian resident owners

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US resident form a US LLC?

Yes. There is no citizenship or residency requirement to own a US LLC or corporation.

Delaware or Wyoming – which is better?

Delaware suits companies planning to raise venture capital; Wyoming is often simpler and cheaper for small, closely held LLCs.

Do Indian residents need any approval?

Investment by Indian residents in an overseas entity is governed by the FEMA Overseas Investment rules. We help you complete the required reporting through your bank.
